About Me

Compassionate Care, Expert Hands

I'm Deepak, a qualified Audiologist & Speech-Language Pathologist holding a Bachelor of Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (BASLP) degree. Based in Chandigarh, I've dedicated my career to improving the communication, hearing, and quality of life of my patients across all age groups.

With over five years of hands-on clinical experience, I specialise in diagnosing and treating a wide spectrum of speech, language, voice, and fluency disorders — alongside comprehensive hearing assessments and audiological rehabilitation. My approach is rooted in evidence-based practice, always tailored to the unique needs of each individual.

Whether it's helping a child find their voice for the first time, supporting a stroke survivor to reclaim their speech, or guiding an adult through hearing aid adjustment — I believe every patient deserves personalised, dignified, and compassionate care.
